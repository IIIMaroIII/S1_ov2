" INDIRECT QUESTIONS — FINAL MENTAL MODEL (EXAM CHEATSHEET)

" --------------------------------------------------
" CORE IDEA (MOST IMPORTANT)
" --------------------------------------------------
An indirect-question sentence may CONTAIN two question IDEAS,
but ONLY ONE clause is a REAL question.

RULE:
- MAIN clause -> follows DIRECT question rules
- EMBEDDED clause -> ALWAYS indirect (statement order)

" --------------------------------------------------
" HOW TO IDENTIFY THE CLAUSES
" --------------------------------------------------

MAIN CLAUSE:
- controls punctuation (?, .)
- controls inversion (aux + subject)
- is what the speaker is REALLY asking

EMBEDDED CLAUSE:
- contains question meaning (who / what / where / why / how / if / whether)
- NEVER uses inversion
- NEVER uses do / does / did

" --------------------------------------------------
" VISUAL MODEL
" --------------------------------------------------

[ MAIN CLAUSE ? ] + [ EMBEDDED QUESTION CONTENT ]

Only the FIRST part behaves like a question.

" --------------------------------------------------
" EXAMPLES (KEY)
" --------------------------------------------------

Example 1:
Did she tell you how often you should take the vitamins?

MAIN:     Did she tell you ... ?   -> DIRECT rules apply
EMBEDDED: how often you should take the vitamins -> STATEMENT order

Example 2:
I don't know where he lives.

MAIN:     I don't know ...         -> statement -> .
EMBEDDED: where he lives           -> STATEMENT order

" --------------------------------------------------
" DIRECT vs INDIRECT — WORD ORDER
" --------------------------------------------------

DIRECT QUESTION:
AUX + SUBJECT + VERB

Why did you call her?
How many times do I have to tell you?

INDIRECT (EMBEDDED):
SUBJECT + VERB

I know why he called her.
I wonder how many times I have to tell you.

" --------------------------------------------------
" AUXILIARY RULE
" --------------------------------------------------

DIRECT:
do / does / did are USED

INDIRECT:
do / does / did are DROPPED

Direct:   Where did she go?
Indirect: I know where she went.

" --------------------------------------------------
" PUNCTUATION RULE
" --------------------------------------------------

Punctuation follows the MAIN clause only.

MAIN = question  -> ?
MAIN = statement -> .

Do you know where she lives?
I don't know where she lives.

" --------------------------------------------------
" SUBJECT QUESTIONS (EASY POINTS)
" --------------------------------------------------

If question word = SUBJECT,
NO inversion in BOTH direct and indirect.

Direct:   Who called you?
Indirect: Do you know who called you?

" --------------------------------------------------
" COMMON EXAM TRAPS
" --------------------------------------------------

WRONG: Could you tell me why are you leaving?
RIGHT: Could you tell me why you are leaving?

WRONG: I don't know where did he go.
RIGHT: I don't know where he went.

WRONG: How many people you invited?
RIGHT: How many people did you invite?

" --------------------------------------------------
" FINAL EXAM MANTRA (LOCK THIS IN)
" --------------------------------------------------

Direct rules apply ONLY to the MAIN clause.
Embedded questions are ALWAYS indirect.
