    to think outside the box
meaning: to think creatively and not follow usual patterns
    to skin a cat
meaning: there are many different ways to achieve the same result
    infeasible
meaning: impossible or unrealistic to do
    ludicrous
meaning: extremely silly or unreasonable
    aliens
meaning: beings from another planet
    to exclude
meaning: to leave something out or remove it from consideration
    outcome
meaning: the final result of an action or decision
    awesome
meaning: extremely good or impressive
    wherewithal
meaning: the resources, ability, or means to do something
    to execute
meaning: to carry out a plan or decision
    to put into motion
meaning: to start something or make it happen
    deficiency
meaning: a lack or weakness in something
    pitfall
meaning: a hidden problem or difficulty
    unforeseen consequences
meaning: unexpected results or effects
    by fiat
meaning: by authority or command, without discussion
    rapport
meaning: a good relationship based on trust and understanding
    to command
meaning: to give orders or have authority over others
