Intro "A motivational talk about developing good habits"

Good afternoon everyone.
Before we begin, let me hook you with a simple fact: most of our daily actions—up to 40%—are habits, not conscious decisions. This means that whether we succeed or deviate from our goals often depends on routines we barely notice.
My name is Rodion, and I’m a first-year Software Engineering student focusing on Java development. As someone learning to build clean code and reliable systems, I’ve realised that good habits matter just as much in life as they do in programming.
Today, I’ll be giving a motivational talk about developing good habits. Let me briefly outline the scope of this presentation.
First, I’ll give a quick breakdown of why habits are powerful.
Second, I’ll share how small, consistent actions create long-term success.
Finally, I’ll finish with practical steps you can start using today.
At the end, I’ll do a short recap so you leave with one clear takeaway you can apply immediately.
This talk will last about five minutes.
My policy on questions is simple: feel free to ask anything you’d like after the presentation, and I’ll be happy to discuss it.
